# SISAP company profile

Source: public information from https://www.sisap.com/ (mock data for this POC).

## About SISAP
SISAP is a cybersecurity company with the tagline "MakeITSecure". It reports more than 41 years of experience in cybersecurity products, services and implementations for large, small and medium-sized enterprises, with offices in Latin America and the U.S. Its solutions protect enterprises from cyber threats using market-leading technology and certified expert knowledge.

## What SISAP does for customers
SISAP protects against cyberattacks and external threats, prevents data leaks and data loss, creates regulatory and compliance plans, prevents unauthorized access to critical resources, and fosters cybersecurity awareness and culture inside companies.

## Business units
SISAP CERT, Outsourcing, Anti-Fraud, Law Enforcement, Service Provider, Identity Access Management (IAM) and OT Security. SISAP also offers cybersecurity training and a certified-professional program.

## Offices
Guatemala, Colombia, Mexico, Panama, Honduras, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Nicaragua, El Salvador and Paraguay.

## Partners
Strategic alliances with global leaders such as Verizon and IBM Corporation.
